(Home Department)
*Maharashtra to be completely free of narcotic drugs by 2029*
• Comprehensive policy as next step under Nasha Mukt Bharat Abhiyan

The State Cabinet, chaired by Chief Minister Devendra Fadnavis, approved a comprehensive policy to eliminate narcotic drugs from Maharashtra by 2029 under the Nasha Mukt Bharat Abhiyan. Four pillars were fixed: enforcement, cutting supply, cutting demand, and de-addiction with treatment and rehabilitation, with responsibilities distributed from village to Mantralaya level. Maharashtra is the first State to run such a whole-of-government campaign.

(Public Works Department)
*Solapur’s Old Pune Naka–Boramani Naka elevated corridor project to get impetus*
• Traffic movement to improve; project accorded urgent public project status

The State Cabinet approved declaring the 9.660 km four-lane elevated corridor from Old Pune Naka to Boramani Naka on National Highway 65 in Solapur an urgent public project. Land held by various government departments will be transferred to the National Highways Authority without occupancy charges, clearing the way for the ₹1,020.50 crore project. It is expected to ease congestion and smooth interstate and pilgrimage traffic.

(Public Health Department)
*Healthy food and balanced menus made mandatory at government and semi-government meetings, programmes and canteens*

The State Cabinet made it mandatory to serve healthy refreshments and balanced menus at government and semi-government meetings, programmes, training institutions and canteens. Seasonal fruit, boiled eggs and pulses, poha, idli-sambar, sugar-free green tea, ragi and jowar bhakri, buttermilk and salad will be prioritised, while oily and highly processed foods are reduced. New catering contracts must include a healthy menu.

(Water Resources Department)
*₹5,704 crore allocation for Kukadi Project*
• Irrigation benefits to 1,44,912 hectares in Pune, Ahilyanagar and Solapur districts

The State Cabinet approved the fourth revised administrative approval for the Kukadi Irrigation Project in Pune district, sanctioning ₹5,704.50 crore. The joint project of five dams—Yedgaon, Manikdoh, Vadaj, Dimbhe and Pimpalgaon Joge—will provide irrigation to 1,44,912 hectares across seven drought-prone talukas in Pune, Ahilyanagar and Solapur districts. Of this, ₹4,946.20 crore is for works and ₹758.30 crore for allied costs.

(गृह विभाग)
*महाराष्ट्र से वर्ष २०२९ तक मादक पदार्थों का पूरी तरह उन्मूलन*
• नशामुक्त भारत अभियान के तहत अगला कदम, व्यापक नीति को मंजूरी

मुख्यमंत्री देवेंद्र फडणवीस की अध्यक्षता में हुई मंत्रिमंडल बैठक में वर्ष २०२९ तक राज्य से मादक पदार्थों के पूर्ण उन्मूलन के लिए व्यापक नीति को मंजूरी दी गई। प्रभावी क्रियान्वयन, आपूर्ति में कमी, मांग में कमी तथा नशामुक्ति-उपचार-पुनर्वास ये चार स्तंभ निर्धारित कर ग्राम स्तर से मंत्रालय स्तर तक जिम्मेदारियां बांटी गई हैं। ऐसा व्यापक अभियान चलाने वाला महाराष्ट्र देश का पहला राज्य है।
